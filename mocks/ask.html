Because a Friday deploy caused the worst outage of the year. On 13 March a billing migration took route planning down for EU customers for 3 h 12 m, and the one weekend on-call engineer could not roll it back alone1. In the postmortem Priya Raman ruled that nothing goes to production on a Friday2. Since June, Sev1 hotfixes are allowed if the on-call lead signs off3.

Note: the "Release process" wiki page says the opposite. Canon has flagged it as stale.

How well does it work?

We hid known decisions, changes and conflicts in Northwind's 259 items, then checked how many Canon found on its own. These are the results of the last run, on 17 October 2026.

  1. 36 of 38

    Found 36 of 38 decisions

    From messy chats and transcripts, Canon pulled out almost every real decision, with who made it and when.

  2. 16 of 17

    Caught 16 of 17 changes and contradictions

    When a new message changed or went against an old decision, Canon noticed. It raised 3 false alarms.

  3. 8 of 8

    Found all 8 out-of-date documents

    Every wiki page that still describes an old decision was flagged.

  4. 14 of 15

    Gave agents the right answer 14 of 15 times

    Allow, warn or block. The one miss was too careful: it blocked a Friday deploy to a test system.

  5. 5 of 5

    Said "I do not know" to all 5 questions it could not answer

    It did not make anything up when the record had no answer.

  6. 1.1 s

    A typical answer in 1.1 seconds

    Half of all questions and checks came back faster than this. 95 in 100 came back within 1.4 seconds.

  7. 184

    Every one of 184 quotes checked word for word

    Canon keeps a quote only if it appears exactly in the source. No invented quotes.

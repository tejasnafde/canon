Answered in 1.1 seconds

Northwind used to release on Friday afternoons. That was a trial slot from January 2025, chosen because support tickets were quiet then.1

On 6 March 2026 a Friday rollout stopped dispatch for 47 minutes. The rollback engineer was on a flight and the backup person was on a customer call. Three days later the team banned production deploys on Fridays, including scheduled jobs and config-only changes. Mei Tan owns the rule. Normal releases moved to Tuesday and Thursday mornings.2

Emergency fixes are still allowed on a Friday, but only with approval from the incident commander and two people online.2 The Release process wiki page still shows the old Friday window.3

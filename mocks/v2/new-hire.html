5 things you must know

1

No production deploys on Fridays. This includes scheduled jobs and config-only changes.

Why: a Friday rollout on 6 March 2026 stopped dispatch for 47 minutes, and nobody was free to roll it back.

Route Lens costs USD 149 per workspace per month for new customers.

Why: each shipment needs a second AI pass, so the old USD 99 price lost money. Signed pilots keep USD 99 for six months.

Meeting Route Lens commercial reset · 2 Apr 2026

Route Lens launches on 2 June 2026, not 15 April.

Why: the Jakarta pilot passed only one of the three good weeks it needs before launch.

Meeting Route Lens launch replan · 9 Apr 2026
4

Sakura's data and its backups stay in Japan.

Why: Sakura's procurement contract covers backups, not just the main copy. A backup in Singapore breaks it.

Meeting Sakura residency review · 20 Feb 2025

In a major outage, tell the customer within 30 minutes, even if you do not know the cause yet.

Why: Sakura once heard about an outage from a truck driver before they heard from us.

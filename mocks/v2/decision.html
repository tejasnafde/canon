No production deploys on Fridays, including scheduled jobs and configuration-only releases.

OwnerMei Tan, engineering manager
Decided9 March 2026
WhereINC-042 review meeting
ReplacesThe Friday release window (Jan 2025)

Why

A Friday rollout on 6 March 2026 stopped dispatch for 47 minutes. The rollback engineer was on a flight and the backup person was on a customer call. The job that caused it ran on its own, so the ban covers scheduled jobs too.

"The 6 March rollout at 15:10 SGT stalled dispatch acknowledgements for 47 minutes. The rollback engineer was on a flight and the secondary was in a customer call."

Exception: an emergency fix, with approval from the incident commander and two people online. Normal releases moved to Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

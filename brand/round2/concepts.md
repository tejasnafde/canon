# Canon logo, round 2: the ribbon family

Built by `python build.py` in this folder (reuses `../build.py` and the path wordmark).
Each variant folder holds `symbol.svg`, `lockup.svg` and `icon.svg`. `concepts.png` compares
all six with the round 1 Ribbon (row 7). Vermilion is used only for the mark that matters.

1. **The Kept C** (`kept-c/`). An open C holds a vermilion ribbon in its mouth.
   The name and the kept place are one shape: the ribbon closes the C, so nothing falls out.
2. **Stop Tail** (`stop-tail/`). The round 1 page and ribbon, but the tail that hangs past the page is vermilion.
   The notch is the stop: the part you see first is the warning before a decision is broken.
3. **The Stack** (`stack/`). Pages seen edge-on with one vermilion ribbon tucked under the first.
   The record is many pages; one decision is marked, and it runs over everything after it.
4. **The Volume** (`volume/`). A closed book (spine, cover, page block) with a ribbon out of the pages.
   The canon as a book you can close and still find your place in. Literal, and the least ownable.
5. **The Drape** (`drape/`). One ribbon draped over a page edge, a long front tail and a short back tail.
   No page drawn, only the ribbon. Ownable silhouette, but it can read as an "n" and loses the accent.
6. **Two Strands** (`strands/`). Two ribbons from one headband, one ink, one vermilion.
   People and agents read from the same memory and keep the same place. Distinct, but close to a "T" or "pi".

## Recommendation

**The Kept C.** It is the only variant that carries the name, it holds up at 16 px on light and dark
(a C plus one red bar), and the vermilion has one job: the kept decision. Second choice: The Stack,
which tells the "record with one decision marked" story best but turns to stripes at 16 px.

# Canon logo concepts

Contact sheet: `concepts.png`. Each concept folder holds `symbol.svg`, `lockup.svg`
(symbol + "Canon" in Fraunces, converted to paths) and `icon.svg` (app icon).
Colours: paper `#f6f1e7`, ink `#1c1a17`, vermilion `#c8361e` only for block or drift.
Rebuild: `python wordmark.py <fraunces woff2>` once, then `python build.py`.

1. **The Record** - a ledger: a margin rule, three entries, and one entry marked.
   It says "one written record", and the vermilion mark is the entry that blocks.
2. **The Seal** - a stamp edge with a C cut out of the middle.
   It reads as "decided and sealed": the authority of the canon, with no accent needed.
3. **The Ribbon** - a page with a bookmark ribbon.
   The company's place is kept; the ribbon marks what was decided so nobody loses it.
4. **The Stop** - a heavy C followed by a vermilion full stop.
   "Canon. Full stop." The decision is final, and the stop is where an agent gets blocked.
5. **The Drift** - two parallel rules; the second breaks and its end slips out of line.
   It is the product in one glyph: the canon, and the drift that Canon catches.
6. **Keystone** - an arch of stones held by one wedge that stands proud.
   One decision that everything else rests on; mono only, so it is the calmest option.

Recommendation: **The Drift** or **The Stop**. Both stay clear at 16px, both use the
accent for exactly the product's job (drift, block), and neither leans on a C that
can read as a copyright sign.
